# Commercial Coverage Lines

Use this as a line-selection aid, not a rating guide. Confirm appetite and
forms with the market.

## Core package

| Line | Typical trigger | Data to collect | Common broker asks |
|---|---|---|---|
| General liability | Premises and operations, products, completed ops | ops description, payroll/sales, subcontracted cost, limits required by contract | additional insured, PNC, waiver, per-project aggregate |
| Commercial property | Buildings, BPP, BI/EE | SOV, COPE, values, occupancy, protective safeguards | agreed value, replacement cost, ordinance or law, flood/quake |
| Commercial auto | Owned, hired, non-owned | VIN schedule, drivers, radius, use | MCS-90 if motor carrier, additional insured, hired/non-owned |
| Workers compensation | Employees, 3.A states | class codes, payroll, experience mod, officers | other-states, waiver, alternate employer |
| Umbrella / excess | Over GL, auto, employers liability | underlying schedule, losses, contracts | follow-form vs broader, additional insured, SIR |

## Common add-ons

| Line | Typical trigger | Watch items |
|---|---|---|
| Inland marine | Tools, installation, contractors equipment, cargo | unattended theft, borrowed equipment, jobsites |
| Crime / fidelity | Employee theft, social engineering, ERISA | social-engineering sublimits, vendor impersonation |
| Cyber | Network, ransomware, privacy | waiting period, ransomware coinsurance, retro date |
| Professional / E&O | Advice for a fee, design, consulting | claims-made, retro, insured vs insured |
| D&O / EPL / fiduciary | Management decisions, employment practices | Side A/B/C, hammer clause, third-party EPL |
| Pollution / contractors pollution | Environmental ops, jobsite pollution | gradual vs sudden, transportation, mold |
| Builders risk | Construction project | soft costs, delay, existing structures, occupancy |

## Account-type hints

- **Contractors**: GL + inland marine + auto + WC + umbrella; track
  subcontractor agreements and certificates received.
- **Manufacturers**: products/completed ops, product recall discussion,
  worldwide coverage if shipping abroad.
- **Real estate / habitational**: property COPE, habitability, assault and
  battery, animal liability, ordinance or law.
- **Professional services**: E&O plus GL/hired-non-owned; watch dual GL/E&O
  claims.
- **Trucking**: auto liability, physical damage, cargo, motor truck cargo vs
  shippers interest; filings and radius.

Match requested contract insurance exhibits to these lines before marketing.
