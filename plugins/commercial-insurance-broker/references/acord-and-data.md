# ACORD and Submission Data

Collect only what the selected lines need. Mark each item `have`, `missing`,
or `not applicable`.

## Universal account data

- Legal named insured and any DBAs or additional named insureds
- Mailing and physical addresses; website
- FEIN / tax ID if the agency requires it for the AMS record
- Years in business, years of management experience
- Description of operations and percentage split by activity
- Target effective date and incumbent carrier/premium if known
- Prior cancellations, non-renewals, and coverage lapses
- Loss runs, currently valued, typically 3–5 years plus current year
- Large-loss narratives for claims over the agency threshold
- Contract insurance requirements or sample exhibits

## Line-specific (common ACORD mapping)

| Need | Typical form / schedule |
|---|---|
| Applicant and first named insured | ACORD 125 |
| Commercial general liability | ACORD 126 |
| Commercial property / COPE / SOV | ACORD 140, statement of values |
| Crime | ACORD 141 or carrier supplement |
| Transportation / motor truck cargo | ACORD 143 or carrier application |
| Garage and dealers | ACORD 128 when applicable |
| Commercial auto | ACORD 127, 129, 137, vehicle and driver schedules |
| Workers compensation | ACORD 130, state-specific pages, NCCI/WCIRB data |
| Umbrella | ACORD 131 and underlying worksheet |
| Cyber / professional / D&O | carrier applications, not generic ACORD alone |

Exact ACORD editions vary by agency and state. Name the data needed rather
than asserting a specific edition unless the user provides it.

## Submission quality bar

A marketable submission usually includes:

1. Complete named-insured and operations story
2. Currently valued loss runs or a written explanation of why none exist
3. Exposure base (sales, payroll, units, vehicle count, TIV)
4. Controls (safety, quality, driver, cyber, subcontractor)
5. Target coverage structure and contractual insurance asks
6. Desired timeline and who can answer underwriter questions

Reject "send to market" language until blocking items in that list are
addressed or explicitly waived by the producer.
