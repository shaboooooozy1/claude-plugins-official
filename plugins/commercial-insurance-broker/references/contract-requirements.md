# Contract Insurance Requirements

Use when reviewing owner, GC, vendor, or landlord insurance exhibits.
Map each ask to a coverage grant. Do not treat a certificate as coverage.

## Extraction checklist

Pull these fields from the exhibit before comparing to the policy:

- Additional insured: ongoing operations, completed operations, or both
- Primary and noncontributory
- Waiver of subrogation
- Notice of cancellation (30/60 days; 10 days for non-pay is common)
- Per-project or per-location aggregate
- Limits by line, including umbrella following form
- Workers compensation waiver and alternate employer
- Auto: any auto vs scheduled; hired/non-owned
- Professional, pollution, or cyber if the work includes those exposures
- Manuscript wording that copies policy language onto the certificate

## Typical mapping (verify against actual forms)

| Contract ask | Usual policy path | Watch item |
|---|---|---|
| AI ongoing ops | CG 20 10 or carrier equivalent | Named vs blanket; scheduled vs automatic |
| AI completed ops | CG 20 37 or equivalent | Often missing on GL; umbrella may not follow |
| Primary / noncontributory | CG 20 01 or AI endorsement text | Confirm it applies to the holder |
| Waiver of subrogation | CG 24 04 (GL), WC waiver, auto waiver | Must exist on each line requested |
| Per-project aggregate | CG 25 03 or equivalent | Needed when many jobs share one aggregate |
| Umbrella additional insured | Follow-form vs broader form | Schedule of underlying; SIR vs deductible |
| 30-day notice on certificate | Policy cancellation condition | Certificates cannot create notice rights |

Mark each row `supported`, `endorsement-required`, `cannot-meet`, or
`unverified`. `cannot-meet` items belong in a contract-negotiation list,
not on a certificate.

## Red flags

- Certificate wording that amends the policy
- Additional insured for the sole negligence of the holder, if prohibited
- Waiver on lines that are claims-made without confirming retro dates
- Limits above the quoted umbrella that the account has not purchased
- Primary wording on a policy that is excess by form
- Hold-harmless clauses that belong to counsel, not the certificate

## Output

Produce a two-column table: contract language (short quote) vs program
response. End with the minimum endorsement order required before the job
starts.
